# Belt Alignment Helpers

Check vertical belt spacing at the toolhead. Choose the helper that matches your belt width.

![Belt alignment helpers](Images/belt_alignment_helpers.png)

STLs: [6 mm](STLs/6mm_belt_offset_helper.stl) · [9 mm](STLs/9mm_belt_offset_helper.stl) · [10 mm](STLs/10mm_belt_offset_helper.stl) · [12 mm](STLs/12mm_belt_offset_helper.stl)

[Download the STEP assembly](CAD/Belt_offset_helpers.step)

## How to use

Slide the helper between the X-rail and the belts, then rotate it into position to check the spacing. Check both sides of the toolhead.
